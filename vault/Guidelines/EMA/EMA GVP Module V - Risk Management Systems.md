# EMA GVP Module V — Risk Management Systems

## Overview

GVP Module V defines requirements for the Risk Management Plan (RMP) — the structured document that describes the safety profile of a medicinal product, how safety is monitored, and what measures are taken to minimize identified risks. An RMP is required for all new marketing authorisations in the EU and for significant regulatory events (new indication, major change, generic/hybrid applications).

## Risk Management Plan Structure

The EU RMP has seven parts:

### Part I — Product Overview
- Drug substance, formulation, indication(s), target population
- Regulatory history and development status

### Part II — Safety Specification
**Module SII**: Non-clinical part (toxicology, pharmacology, reproductive toxicity)
**Module SIII**: Clinical trial exposure (exposure by age, sex, race, dose)
**Module SIV**: Populations not studied in clinical trials
**Module SV**: Post-authorisation experience (if any)
**Module SVI**: Additional EU requirements
**Module SVII**: Identified and potential risks (summary table)
**Module SVIII**: Summary of safety concerns

**Safety concern categories**:
- **Identified risk**: Evidence of a causal association (from trials, epidemiology, or post-marketing)
- **Potential risk**: Plausible mechanism or signal that has not been confirmed
- **Missing information**: Gaps in knowledge (e.g., use in pregnancy, paediatric patients, severe renal impairment)

### Part III — Pharmacovigilance Plan
Specifies routine and additional PV activities to characterize safety concerns:
- **Routine**: Spontaneous ADR monitoring, periodic safety reporting, literature monitoring
- **Additional**: Post-authorisation safety studies (PASS), registries, cohort studies, case-control studies

### Part IV — Plans for Post-Authorisation Efficacy Studies (PAES)
For conditional MAs, ATMP, or where efficacy gaps need confirmation.

### Part V — Risk Minimisation Measures
**Routine risk minimisation**:
- Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC)
- Package leaflet
- Labeling
- Dispensing/prescribing restrictions (prescription-only vs. OTC)

**Additional risk minimisation measures (aRMMs)**:
- Healthcare professional educational materials (DHPC, training materials)
- Patient/caregiver educational materials, alert cards
- Controlled access programs (similar to US REMS)
- Pregnancy prevention programs
- Prescription/dispensing restriction (e.g., specialist only)

## Risk Minimisation Effectiveness

PRAC may require MAHs to assess the effectiveness of additional risk minimisation measures via:
- Process indicators (prescribing rates in unauthorized populations)
- Outcome indicators (clinical event rates)
- Surveys (HCP awareness, patient knowledge)
- Drug Utilisation Studies (DUS)

## Key PRAC Assessments for RMPs

The PRAC evaluates RMPs as part of initial MA applications and for significant variations. Key PRAC criteria:
- Are the safety concerns correctly identified and categorized?
- Is the pharmacovigilance plan proportionate to the risks?
- Are proposed risk minimisation measures effective and necessary?
- Does the risk-benefit balance remain positive with the proposed RMP?

## RMP Updates

MAHs must update the RMP when:
- New safety information becomes available
- New risk minimisation measures are implemented
- An important milestone in the PV plan is reached
- A variation affecting the safety profile is submitted

**Version control**: RMP versions are tracked by version number; all submitted versions become public (via EPAR).

## Relationship to Other GVP Modules

- **Module VI**: Adverse reaction reporting provides case data informing safety concerns
- **Module VIII**: PASS design and conduct addresses uncharacterized risks in the PV plan
- **Module IX**: Signal management identifies new potential risks that may require RMP update
- **Module XVI**: Risk minimisation measures implementation and effectiveness

## Comparison with FDA Risk Management

| Aspect | EU RMP | US REMS |
|---|---|---|
| Legal basis | EU Regulation 726/2004 | FDA FDAAA 2007 |
| Scope | Comprehensive risk management document | Targeted risk mitigation strategies |
| Required for | All new EU MAs | Only when necessary to ensure benefits outweigh risks |
| Pharmacovigilance plan | Integrated into RMP | Separate requirement |
| Public availability | Key RMP summary published in EPAR | REMS documentation public on FDA website |
| Regulatory review | PRAC | FDA CDER/CBER |
