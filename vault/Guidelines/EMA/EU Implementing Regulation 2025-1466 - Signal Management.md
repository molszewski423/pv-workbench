# EU Implementing Regulation 2025/1466 — Signal Management System Reform

## Overview

The European Commission adopted **Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1466** on **22 July 2025**, amending Implementing Regulation (EU) No 520/2012. The regulation reforms the EU pharmacovigilance signal management framework with the primary goals of:

1. Strengthening patient safety through an improved signal management system
2. Increasing transparency in subcontracting of pharmacovigilance activities
3. Clarifying signal management roles between MAHs and NCAs/EMA

## Key Changes

### Signal Notification Process

**Previous process (pilot scheme, now ended):**
- MAHs submitted validated signals to EMA/NCAs via a standalone signal notification form
- Separate notification was expected for each validated signal

**New process (effective 22 July 2025):**
- Standalone signal notification forms are **no longer required or expected**
- MAHs handle validated signals entirely within their own signal management process per **GVP Module IX**
- Signal outcomes are communicated through the existing periodic safety report (PSUR/PBRER) framework
- EMA/NCAs retain authority to request ad hoc signal information

### EudraVigilance Monitoring Obligations

MAHs must describe monitoring of EudraVigilance data when conducting signal detection. Requirements:
- **Monitoring frequency**: Proportionate to risk level, known safety profile, and product characteristics
- **Risk-based approach**: High-risk products (black triangle ▼, products under additional monitoring) require more frequent review
- Monitoring procedures must be documented in the PSMF and signal management SOPs

### Subcontracting Transparency

New requirements for MAHs that subcontract pharmacovigilance activities:
- Clearly document in PSMF which activities are subcontracted
- Identify the subcontractor (name, location, contact)
- Retain responsibility and oversight for subcontracted signal detection activities
- Ensure subcontractors comply with same GVP standards

### Pharmacovigilance System Requirements Clarification

Regulation clarifies terminology around:
- Qualified Person for Pharmacovigilance (QPPV) responsibilities in signal management
- Definition of "validated signal" for EU purposes
- Interface between national signal management and EMA's centrally coordinated signal detection

## Operational Impact for MAHs

| Area | Action Required |
|---|---|
| SOP updates | Remove standalone signal notification form process |
| PSMF | Document new signal process; update subcontracting section |
| EudraVigilance monitoring | Define monitoring frequency per product risk level |
| PSUR/PBRER | Signal outcomes integrated into periodic reports |
| Training | Update pharmacovigilance staff on new signal workflow |

## Effective Date

**22 July 2025** — Immediate applicability. No transitional period for signal notification form removal.

## Relationship to GVP Module IX (Signal Management)

This regulation operationalises GVP Module IX Rev 1 requirements at the legislative level. GVP Module IX remains the primary guidance document for:
- Signal detection methodologies (PRR, ROR, Bayesian methods)
- Signal validation criteria
- Signal prioritisation and assessment
- Communication of signal outcomes
