# EMA GVP Module I — Pharmacovigilance Systems and Their Quality Systems

## Purpose and Scope

GVP Module I establishes the requirements for the pharmacovigilance system that Marketing Authorisation Holders (MAHs) and the European Medicines Agency (EMA) must maintain for centrally authorised products. It defines the quality framework, key personnel, documentation, and oversight mechanisms that underpin all EU pharmacovigilance activities.

## Pharmacovigilance System Master File (PSMF)

The PSMF is the cornerstone document describing the MAH's pharmacovigilance system. It must be maintained as a living document and available at all times for inspection.

### Mandatory PSMF Content
1. **Location of the QPPV** — name, contact details, CV, qualifications, work arrangements
2. **Structure and activities** — organizational chart, responsibilities, outsourcing arrangements
3. **Data sources and databases** — systems used for case management, signal detection, periodic reporting
4. **Computer systems and databases** — list of safety databases with validation status
5. **Quality system** — SOPs list, training records, audit schedule, deviations log
6. **Contracted activities** — vendors, CROs, third-party PV services with oversight documentation
7. **Product list** — all products covered by the MAH's pharmacovigilance system

### PSMF Location Requirements
- Physical location in the EU or EEA
- Must be the location of the QPPV
- Immediately accessible to competent authorities on request
- Must be registered with EudraVigilance and updated within 30 days of significant changes

## Qualified Person Responsible for Pharmacovigilance (QPPV)

The QPPV is the individual with continuous responsibility for establishing and maintaining the MAH's pharmacovigilance system. This is a legally required role under EU legislation (Directive 2010/84/EU).

### QPPV Responsibilities
- Establish and maintain the PV system
- Be contactable at all times (24/7 availability or named backup)
- Have overview of all safety data for all products in the EU
- Review and approve all periodic safety reports (PSURs/PBRERs)
- Make the pharmacovigilance system available for regulatory inspection
- Notify competent authorities of any safety issue requiring urgent attention

### QPPV Qualifications
- Resident and operating in the EU
- Appropriate qualifications (medical, scientific, or pharmacy background)
- Demonstrated PV training and experience
- Backed by a deputy QPPV for absence coverage

## Quality System Requirements

### Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)
MAHs must maintain documented SOPs covering all pharmacovigilance activities:
- Individual case safety report (ICSR) handling and submission
- Signal detection and management
- Periodic safety reporting (PSUR/PBRER)
- Risk management plan (RMP) preparation and maintenance
- Literature monitoring
- Audit and inspection management

### Training Requirements
- All PV staff must have documented training on relevant SOPs
- Training records maintained with date, scope, and trainer
- Annual training records reviewed and archived

### Deviation Management
- All deviations from SOPs must be documented
- Root cause analysis for significant deviations
- CAPA (Corrective and Preventive Actions) tracked to closure

## Pharmacovigilance System Audits

MAHs must conduct periodic audits of their pharmacovigilance systems:
- **Internal audits**: Minimum every 2 years, following a risk-based audit plan
- **Audits of outsourced vendors**: Regular oversight including audit rights in contracts
- **Audit reports**: Document findings, CAPAs, and completion status
- **Audit program oversight**: QPPV review and approval required

## EMA's Pharmacovigilance System

The EMA maintains its own pharmacovigilance system including:
- **EudraVigilance**: Central EU safety database for ICSR receipt and signal detection
- **PRAC**: Committee for monitoring safety of medicinal products for human use
- **EVDAS**: EudraVigilance Data Analysis System for signal detection using BCPNN methodology
- **EPAR**: European Public Assessment Reports — public safety assessments
- **Signal detection**: Weekly automated signal detection on EU ICSRs

## Key Legal Basis

- **Directive 2001/83/EC** (as amended by 2010/84/EU) — human medicines framework
- **Regulation (EU) No 726/2004** — centrally authorised products
- **Implementing Regulation (EU) No 520/2012** — PV activities detail
- **Commission Regulation (EU) No 1235/2010** — PV requirements for centrally authorised products
